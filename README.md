## 📌 Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Tata Group Data Visualisation Virtual Experience Program** on The Forage. The objective was to simulate a real-world business intelligence scenario at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), where an analyst transforms raw corporate sales data into meaningful visual insights for senior decision-makers.

The dataset includes sales revenue, quantity sold, and customer transactions across multiple countries and months. All dashboards were built using **Power BI Desktop** and compiled into a structured report.

## 📈 Dashboard Summaries

### 1. Monthly Revenue Analysis
A line chart displaying revenue across all 12 months.

| Highlight | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Highest Revenue Month | March – 1.46M |
| Second Highest | November – 1.07M |
| Third Highest | October – 1.02M |
| Lowest Revenue Month | February – 0.43M |

**Key Insight:** A sharp spike in March suggests seasonal demand, a major promotion, or a significant project completion. Dips in February, May, and August indicate areas requiring strategic attention.

---

### 2. Country-wise Revenue & Quantity
A combined bar chart showing total quantity sold and revenue per country.

| Country | Quantity | Revenue |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Netherlands | 197K | 279K |
| EIRE (Ireland) | 141K | 259K |
| Australia | 77K | 124K |
| France | 37K | 32K |

**Key Insight:** Strong market presence in Western Europe and Oceania. Countries like Bahrain, Portugal, Switzerland, and Israel show negligible contributions — signalling opportunity for targeted growth strategies.

---

### 3. Customer-wise Revenue
A bar chart ranking the top customers by revenue generated.

| Customer ID | Revenue |
|-------------|---------|
| 14646 | 279K |
| 18102 | 256K |
| 17450 | 187K |
| 14911 | 133K |
| 12415 | 124K |

**Key Insight:** Revenue is heavily concentrated among a few key customers. This dependency on major accounts is a risk factor — diversifying the customer base would improve revenue stability.

---

### 4. Geographic Quantity Distribution
A geographic heatmap showing quantity sold by country worldwide.

**Key Insight:** Netherlands and Ireland are the dominant markets. Germany, France, Sweden, and Australia follow. Underperforming regions include Brazil, South Africa, Bahrain, and most of Asia — highlighting untapped market potential.

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard creation and data visualisation

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- March and Q4 are the strongest revenue periods; February is consistently weak.
- Western Europe (Netherlands, Ireland) drives the bulk of sales volume and revenue.
- A small group of customers accounts for a disproportionately large share of revenue — a Pareto-style distribution.
- Geographic expansion into Asia, the Americas, and the Middle East represents significant untapped opportunity.
